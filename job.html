Job Advertisement

Software Engineering Intern — Google

Location: Mountain View, CA (Bay Area)  |  Internship

About the role

Google’s internship programs give students the chance to work on real projects that impact users worldwide. Software Engineering Interns collaborate with experienced engineers, write and test code, and help solve challenging technical problems.

Minimum qualifications

  • Currently pursuing a Bachelor’s degree in Computer Science, Mathematics, or a related technical field
  • Experience with programming concepts through coursework or projects
  • Ability to learn quickly and work in a collaborative team environment

Preferred qualifications

  • Strong problem-solving and analytical thinking skills
  • Clear written and verbal communication; experience working with diverse teams
  • Interest in building reliable software and learning industry best practices
  • Ability to manage multiple priorities and meet deadlines

Responsibilities

  • Contribute to design, development, testing, or documentation of software features
  • Collaborate with mentors and teammates to ship high-quality work
  • Communicate progress, ask questions, and incorporate feedback

Dear Hiring Manager,

I am writing to apply for the Software Engineering Intern role at Google. I am a student at San Diego State University pursuing a B.S. in Mathematics with an Emphasis in Computational Science, and I previously earned an A.S. in Computer Science for Transfer at De Anza College. Google’s focus on solving hard problems at scale—and doing so in collaborative teams—matches the kind of internship I want.

My academic path has built a foundation in programming concepts and analytical problem solving. I approach technical work the same way I approach math: break a challenge into parts, test ideas carefully, and explain the solution clearly. I am eager to grow those habits under Google mentors while contributing to real projects.

Outside the classroom, I have led and supported teams under pressure. As JV Head Volleyball Coach and as an Assistant Coach at De Anza College, I made quick decisions during games, managed schedules and logistics, and communicated clearly with diverse players and staff—including as a bilingual Spanish/English liaison. In hospitality at Joey Restaurants, I learned to prioritize multiple demands without losing attention to detail. Those experiences show how I collaborate, stay organized, and stay calm when the pace is high.

I would welcome the chance to bring my computational background, work ethic, and teamwork skills to Google’s Bay Area internship program. Thank you for your time and consideration.

Sincerely,
Hector Hernandez Reyes
