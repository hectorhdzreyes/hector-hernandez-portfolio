Job Skills

Four skills I developed through school, coaching, and work—and how they transfer to an internship.

1. Analytical Problem Solving

Developed at: De Anza College (Computer Science) & SDSU (Computational Mathematics)

Through coursework in computer science and mathematics with a computational science emphasis, I practice breaking problems into steps, testing ideas carefully, and explaining solutions clearly. This habit of structured thinking helps me approach technical tasks with patience and accuracy.

2. Leadership & Decision-Making Under Pressure

Developed at: University Prep Academy (JV Head Coach) & De Anza College (Assistant Coach)

As a head coach, I designed practices, managed lineups, and made live in-game adjustments. In high-pressure matches I had to decide quickly, stay calm, and keep the team focused. Those same skills matter when prioritizing work and supporting teammates on a technical project.

3. Bilingual Communication

Developed at: De Anza College coaching staff & daily professional interactions

Fluent in Spanish and English, I have translated instructions and helped players, staff, and peers understand each other. Clear communication across language and culture is valuable on diverse teams—especially in customer-facing or collaborative tech environments.

4. Time Management & Multitasking

Developed at: Joey Restaurants Valley Fair & coaching logistics roles

In restaurant service and coaching, I juggled schedules, travel logistics, equipment, and last-minute changes while keeping standards high. I am used to prioritizing tasks, staying organized, and delivering reliable results when several demands compete for attention.
